Wise Cascade Time Calculator

The Wise Cascades Theorem says that for any problem, q, there exist a level of τ and N which guarantee the community will enter an information cascade with the correct answer with arbitrarily high reliability.

This app shows what level of τ is required to achieve the desired level of reliability, and as a result, how many agents are expected to be required before an information cascade begins.
